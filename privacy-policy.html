Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Zen ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal finance application developed by Zenvy Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Zen app. By using Zen, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Operating Modes

Zen operates in two modes with different data practices:

  • Guest Mode — No account is required. Expenses stay entirely on your device. No email, phone number, or name is collected. No data is sent to our servers.
  • Authenticated Mode — Requires an email address, phone number, and display name to enable shared features such as expense splitting and task collaboration. Data is stored in Firebase Firestore.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Zen in Authenticated Mode, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address, phone number, display name
  • Expense data — amounts, merchants, categories, dates, and transaction references you confirm
  • Task data — task titles, assignments, due dates, and status changes
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, for crash diagnostics

Raw source data is never sent to our servers. SMS text bodies, receipt images, and bank statement files are processed on-device only. Only the structured expense fields you confirm are stored.

3. Gmail Access

If you enable Gmail Scanning, Zen requests the gmail.readonly OAuth scope to read transactional emails from your connected Google account. This access is used solely to detect bank and payment alerts and convert them into expense candidates for your review.

  • Email bodies are processed on-device and never transmitted to our servers.
  • Only the structured expense data you explicitly confirm is stored.
  • You can revoke this access at any time via Google Account settings or by disabling Gmail Scanning in the app.
  • We do not read, store, or share the content of any email other than extracting financial transaction amounts, dates, and merchant names.

Our use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

Zen uses the following Google and Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication — secure sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore — cloud storage for tasks, shared expenses, and user profiles
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymised usage analytics (opt-out available in Settings)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting (opt-out available in Settings)
  • Google Gemini API — AI-powered insights and natural language search (data is anonymised before processing)

Each service is contractually bound to use your data only to provide their service to us and in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only:

  • With other Zen users you explicitly invite to a shared expense or task
  • With service providers listed above, solely to operate the app
  • When required by law

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — export all your expense data as CSV or PDF from Settings → Data Backup & Import
  • Delete — delete your account from the app; server data is removed within 30 days
  • Opt out — disable analytics and crash reporting in Settings → Smart AI Features
  • Portability — export your data at any time in machine-readable format

EU residents (GDPR) and California residents (CCPA) may exercise additional rights by contacting us at privacy@zenvytechno.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed from our servers within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption in transit and Firebase security rules to restrict data access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Zen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Zen after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, data requests, or security issues, contact us at:
privacy@zenvytechno.com
